# Language and terminology rules

## Language applicability

Apply the Language & Meaning Core of the packaged AI DevMode Standards (ADS) to communication in all supported languages.

For English, apply project-authorized ASD-STE100 Issue 9 vocabulary and construction rules to applicable agent-authored prose.

For other languages, apply the same communication objectives: clear meaning, consistent terminology, direct construction, explicit references, and controlled ambiguity. Adapt these objectives to the grammar, vocabulary, sentence structure, and writing conventions of each language. Apply English-specific vocabulary and grammar restrictions only to English prose.

Use authorized project terminology for each language. Preserve protected content, technical identifiers, and protocol tokens.

Report communication-quality findings in the active exchange. The designated language review process maintains formal ASD-STE100 conformance evidence for applicable English prose.

## ASD-STE100 language review

Before you assess agent-authored English for ASD-STE100 conformance, consult the project-authorized Issue 9 writing rules and controlled dictionary. Review general vocabulary for approved words, meanings, and parts of speech. Review technical nouns and verbs against the authorized terminology and applicable STE categories. Apply the writing rules for sentence structure and style.

Report the assessment and relevant findings in the active exchange. Identify each required reference that was unavailable during the review. A formal conformance result requires the designated review process and its evidence.

## Language and meaning controls

1. Use authorized ASD-STE100 Issue 9 vocabulary and construction rules for applicable English prose. Obtain designated language-review evidence for formal conformance.
2. Apply the relevant ISO, IEC, and IEEE profiles through the packaged Language & Meaning Core.
3. Use BCP 14 semantics for uppercase normative keywords in normative provisions.
4. Use a single preferred term for each controlled concept. Read `references/dictionary.md` for skill-specific definitions.
5. Use authorized project-specific dictionaries when the active project provides them. Their location and format follow project policy.
6. Consult the packaged ADS terminology where a common concept is defined or where a definition conflict must be resolved.
7. Distinguish facts, assumptions, decisions, requirements, and evidence.
8. State actors, actions, objects, scope, and relevant conditions when these determine meaning.
9. Use positive construction and short, direct instructions.
10. Preserve imported and protected text, technical identifiers, mathematical expressions, commands, and machine syntax.
11. Identify Examples and Illustrations explicitly and keep them explanatory.

Use `standards/ads/language-core/LMC-04_language_and_meaning_conformance.md` during designated formal language reviews.
